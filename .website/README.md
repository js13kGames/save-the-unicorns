# 🦄 Save the Unicorns

**My js13kgames 2026 entry — Vanilla JS · HTML5 Canvas · Zero dependencies · Under 13,312 bytes**

> *A story-based browser game about unicorns and horses.*

## Premise

Your hat stall sits in the town square in the Horses' Land, You expect ordinary horse customers — but some of them aren't horses at all. Unicorns come to you in secret, hunted and desperate, and it's on you to help them look like "a normal horse" before they reach the checkpoint and try to cross the border.

Use the hats and items you have on hand to save the unicorns' lives. Get the disguise right, and they make it home. Get it wrong, and it costs them everything.

## How It Plays

- **The Stall** — the story unfolds day by day. Who's arrived today, and what do they need from you?
- **The Disguise** — pick a hat to hide the horn, a dye for the mane, a coat to match. Every detail has to line up, or the guards will notice.
- **The Crossing** — hold your breath as they walk the checkpoint. Not every disguise holds up, and not every unicorn makes it through.

## Controls

Mouse click / Space - to advance dialogue and confirm choices. No additional controls required.

## Run It

Open `index.html` in any modern browser. That's it — no additional steps needed.
